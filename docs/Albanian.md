## Overview of Albanian Dialects

- **Gheg** — Northern Albania, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Montenegro — nasal vowels and distinct vowel lengths.
- **Tosk** — Southern Albania, parts of Greece and Italy — basis for modern Standard Albanian.
